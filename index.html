      How to use Shades of Grey
      • Units holds everything you own. Add a unit with + Add unit; the number box is how many models are in it.
      • Press ▲ to move a unit up a stage. Made a mistake? Press Undo on the message that pops up.
      • Tap a unit's name to open its details: nickname, model count, repaint flag, paint notes, a photo, back a stage, and delete.
      • Add the same unit twice (same name, game and faction) and they're numbered for you: Intercessors 1, Intercessors 2.
      • Dashboard counts models, not units. Tap any number, chart row or faction to jump to those units.
      • Your stages are yours to change below. Done starts at decides what counts as finished.
      • Everything is saved on this device only. Save a backup regularly, especially before clearing your browser.

      Stages

      Rename, reorder (↑ ↓), add or remove stages. Units move with their stage when you reorder. Changes apply when you press Save.
